About me

I'm an AI developer at Kode Creators Private Limited, where I build products on top of large language models: chatbots that answer from a company's own documents, and agents that call tools to calculate, look things up and act.

I got here through mobile apps and classical machine learning. I shipped Flutter apps, then trained and deployed a crop recommendation model. That path is why I like owning a feature end to end: the retrieval pipeline, the prompts, the API and the deploy.
